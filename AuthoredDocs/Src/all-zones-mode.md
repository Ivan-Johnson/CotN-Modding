# All zones mode

For players. How an all-zones run differs from a single-zone run: its levels,
its bosses, and what you start with.

## Levels

* As Cadence, the run is five zones of four levels: three regular levels and
  a boss level, 1-1 to 1-4, then 2-1 to 2-4, and so on to 5-4. A fifth level
  in zone 5, 5-5, holds the final boss, and beating it ends the run. How each
  level's stairs work: [stairs-and-levels.md](./stairs-and-levels.md).
* As [Dove](./dove.md), the run is the same five zones with no boss levels:
  1-1 to 1-3 up to 5-3, which ends the run.
* You keep your health, gold and items from one zone into the next.
* Which shrines a level can hold differs from a single-zone run:
  [shrines.md](./shrines.md).

## Bosses

* The bosses of zones 1 to 4 are four different ones of these five: King
  Conga ([king-conga.md](./king-conga.md)), Death Metal, Deep Blues, Coral
  Riff and Fortissimole. Which four, and in which order, differs between
  runs.
* Zone 5's boss on 5-4 is always Dead Ringer, and the final boss on 5-5 is
  always the NecroDancer.

## Starting kit

* Cadence and Dove each start with 6 health (3 hearts), against 12 and 14
  in a single-zone run ([cadence.md](./cadence.md), [dove.md](./dove.md)).
* Each starts with only their normal items: Cadence with `Bomb`,
  `WeaponDagger` and `ShovelBasic`; Dove with `Bomb`, `WeaponFlower`,
  `ShovelCrystal` and `RingPeace`. The extra chest and coin items of a
  single-zone run are absent, so gold per kill has no coin-item bonus
  ([coin-multiplier.md](./coin-multiplier.md)).
