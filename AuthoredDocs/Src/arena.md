# Arena

For players. The secret room where you take a free item and then fight four
tough enemies. Applies to Cadence in Single Zone mode. Finding the room, its
cracked wall and the travel rune that takes you there and back:
[secret-shops.md](./secret-shops.md).

## The room

* A closed room of 5 by 7 floor tiles. The travel rune back to the level is
  in the middle of the second row from one end.
* Three items lie in a row across the middle of the room, three tiles in
  front of the rune. None has a price.
* The room holds no enemies until you take an item.

## Taking an item

* Taking an item costs nothing. The other two vanish at once, so you get one
  of the three.
* Taking it starts the fight: four enemies appear in the room. They are one
  of these three groups:

| Group | Enemies |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Blue Banshee (`Banshee`), Black Armored Skeleton (`Armoredskeleton3`), Yellow Skeleton (`Skeleton2`), Blood Nightmare (`Nightmare2`) |
| 2 | Blue Dragon (`Dragon3`), Blue Bat (`Bat`), Yellow Armored Skeleton (`Armoredskeleton2`), Brown DireBat (`BatMiniboss2`) |
| 3 | Red Dragon (`Dragon2`), White Skeleton Mage (`Skeletonmage`), Blue Bat (`Bat`), Dark Minotaur (`Minotaur2`) |

## During the fight

* The travel rune does not work while any of the four lives: stepping onto
  it leaves you standing on it. The fight is the only way on.
* The fight does not hold the level's stairs: killing the level's
  [miniboss](./minibosses.md) unlocks them as usual
  ([stairs-and-levels.md](./stairs-and-levels.md)), even with the fight
  still going.

## After the fight

* The fight ends when all four are dead. Each drops gold.
* The room then grows three rows longer at the rune's end, and the rune
  moves with it, staying second from that end.
* The rune works again and takes you back to the level.
