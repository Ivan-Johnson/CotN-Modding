# Dove

For players. How playing Dove, the pacifist character, differs from
[Cadence](./cadence.md) in a single-zone run.

## Starting kit

* Dove starts with 14 health (7 hearts), against Cadence's 12.
* She starts with `Bomb`, `WeaponFlower`, `ShovelCrystal` (see
  [digging](./digging.md)) and `RingPeace` instead of Cadence's dagger and
  basic shovel.

## The flower

* The flower does no damage, so Dove cannot kill anything with it.
* Attacking an enemy with it "tickles" it. The first tickle makes it drop
  its gold; later ones drop nothing more. Every tickle
  counts as a kill for the groove chain (see
  [coin-multiplier](./coin-multiplier.md)).
* A tickle knocks some enemies back one tile, among them Skeletons and the
  Minotaur, when the tile behind them is free. Green slimes do not budge.
* Dove stays on her tile when she attacks.
* Enemies still hurt her as usual.

## Bombs

* Dove's bombs teleport every enemy in the blast to somewhere else on the
  level, unhurt; a miniboss too. Otherwise they work as in
  [bombs](./bombs.md): they still break dirt walls, and still cost Dove 4
  health if she stands in the blast.

## Levels

* Stairs locked by a living miniboss open when Dove steps on them, and she
  descends. See [stairs-and-levels](./stairs-and-levels.md) and
  [minibosses](./minibosses.md).
* A single-zone run as Dove is three levels long: there is no boss level.
* Her levels have no monkeys ([monkeys](./monkeys.md)), harpies, trapdoors or
  sarcophagi.
