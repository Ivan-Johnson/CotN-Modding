# Shriner

For players. The secret room where the Shriner sells shrines; it is behind
travel rune type 7 (`TravelRune7`). How to find and enter a secret room, and
where it can appear: [secret-shops.md](./secret-shops.md). Applies to Cadence.

* The Shriner (`Shriner`) stands at the back of a small room. Three
  different shrines stand side by side two tiles in front of him, and the
  travel rune out is two tiles in front of the middle shrine.
* In a single-zone run the three are drawn from nine shrines: Blood, Boss,
  Darkness, Glass, Peace, Risk, Space, Uncertainty and War. The Shrine of
  Chance and the four shrines set off beside them (Pain, Sacrifice, Feast and
  Fire) never appear here.

## Buying a shrine

* Each shrine costs 50 gold, on the first floor of every zone. Walk into one
  to pay and use it; having exactly 50 is enough. With less, nothing happens
  and you keep your gold.
* A shrine here does what it does in a level; each shrine's effect:
  [shrines.md](./shrines.md#the-shrines).
* You get one: using a shrine makes the other two vanish.

## The Shriner

* Your weapon cannot hurt him: attacking him does nothing, and he never
  fights back.
* A bomb kills him. A bomb's blast covers the 3 by 3 around it
  ([bombs.md](./bombs.md#the-blast)), so one dropped between him and the
  shrines also destroys the shrines ([shrines.md](./shrines.md#bombs)); drop
  it beside him instead.
* His death starts a fight: three dragons (`Dragon`, `Dragon2` and `Dragon3`)
  appear in the room.
* From then on the shrines are free, even while the dragons live. You still
  get only one.
* While the dragons live, the travel rune out does not work: stepping onto it
  leaves you standing on it.
