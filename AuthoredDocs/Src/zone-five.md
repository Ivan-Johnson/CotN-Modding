# Zone five

For players. The terrain, traps and enemies on zone five's regular levels in
an all-zones run that [zone one](./zone-one.md) does not cover. Its regular
levels are 5-1 to 5-3; its bosses are on 5-4 and 5-5
([all-zones-mode.md](./all-zones-mode.md)). Previous: [zone
four](./zone-four.md).

* **Terrain:** wire (`Wire`) on every level, and wire doors
  (`WireDoorHorizontal`, `WireDoorVertical`).
* **Traps:** scatter traps (`ScatterTrap`).
* **Enemies:** electric zombies (`ZombieElectric`), electric mages
  (`ElectricMage`, `ElectricMage2`, `ElectricMage3`), devils (`Devil`,
  `Devil2`), evil eyes (`Evileye`, `Evileye2`), gorgons (`Gorgon`,
  `Gorgon2`), orcs (`Orc`, `Orc2`, `Orc3`), skulls (`Skull`, `Skull2`,
  `Skull3`), water balls (`WaterBall`) and wraiths (`Wraith2`).
