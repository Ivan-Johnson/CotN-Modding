# Zone four

For players. The terrain, traps and enemies on zone four's regular levels in
an all-zones run that [zone one](./zone-one.md) does not cover. Its regular
levels are 4-1 to 4-3; its boss level is 4-4
([all-zones-mode.md](./all-zones-mode.md)). Previous: [zone
three](./zone-three.md); next: [zone five](./zone-five.md).

* **Terrain:** ooze (`Ooze`).
* **Traps:** diagonal bounce traps (`BounceTrapUpLeft`, `BounceTrapUpRight`,
  `BounceTrapDownLeft`, `BounceTrapDownRight`) and confusion traps
  (`ConfusionTrap`).
* **Enemies:** sarcophagi (`Sarcophagus`, `Sarcophagus2`, `Sarcophagus3`),
  gargoyles (`Gargoyle`, `Gargoyle2`, `Gargoyle3`), harpies (`Harpy`),
  spiders (`Spider`), sleeping goblins (`SleepingGoblin`), goblin bombers
  (`GoblinBomber`), fire pigs (`FirepigLeft`, `FirepigRight`), liches
  (`Lich`, `Lich2`, `Lich3`), warlocks (`Warlock`, `Warlock2`), blademasters
  (`Blademaster`, `Blademaster2`), ghouls (`Ghoul`), monkeys (`Monkey3`,
  `Monkey4`), bats (`Bat4`), golems (`Golem3`), ogres (`Ogre`), mommies
  (`Mommy`), pixies (`Pixie`) and armadillos (`Armadillo3`).
