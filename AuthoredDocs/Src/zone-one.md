# Zone one

For players. The first zone: the whole of a single-zone run as Cadence, and
the first four levels of an all-zones run. Each section points to the pages
that cover its subject. What later zones add: [zone two](./zone-two.md), [zone
three](./zone-three.md), [zone four](./zone-four.md) and [zone
five](./zone-five.md).

## Levels

* Three regular levels, 1-1 to 1-3, then the boss level, 1-4. A miniboss
  guards each regular level's stairs. Stairs, trapdoors and the boss level:
  [stairs-and-levels.md](./stairs-and-levels.md). The five-zone run:
  [all-zones-mode.md](./all-zones-mode.md).
* Your character and their starting kit: [cadence.md](./cadence.md),
  [dove.md](./dove.md). What each input does: [controls.md](./controls.md).

## Enemies

* The regular levels hold slimes (`Slime`, `Slime2`, `Slime3`), skeletons
  (`Skeleton`, `Skeleton2`, `Skeleton3`), zombies (`Zombie`), monkeys
  (`Monkey`, `Monkey2`), bats (`Bat`), ghosts (`Ghost`) and wraiths
  (`Wraith`). Only in an all-zones run do they also hold `Bat2`. Each type
  has a page: [enemies.md](./enemies.md).
* The stairs guards: [minibosses.md](./minibosses.md). The boss:
  [stairs-and-levels.md](./stairs-and-levels.md#1-4-the-boss-level) and
  [king-conga.md](./king-conga.md).
* Gold per kill: [coin-multiplier.md](./coin-multiplier.md).

## Terrain

* Walls include dirt, stone and catacomb walls, and the shop's walls. Which
  shovel digs which: [digging.md](./digging.md). Bombs blow walls open too:
  [bombs.md](./bombs.md).
* Doors (`DoorHorizontal`, `DoorVertical`) open when you walk into them:
  [controls.md](./controls.md).
* Some levels hold water (`Water`).

## Traps

* The levels hold bounce traps, spike traps (`SpikeTrap`), automatic spike
  traps (`Sync_AutoSpikeTrap`), bomb traps (`BombTrap`), tempo traps
  (`TempoUpTrap`, `TempoDownTrap`), dice traps (`Sync_DiceTrap`), floor
  cracks (`Sync_FloorCrack`) and trapdoors (`Trapdoor`). What each does:
  [traps.md](./traps.md); trapdoors:
  [stairs-and-levels.md](./stairs-and-levels.md#trapdoors).
* Every bounce trap points straight up, down, left or right; none is
  diagonal or omni.
