# Zone three

For players. The terrain, traps and enemies on zone three's regular levels in
an all-zones run that [zone one](./zone-one.md) does not cover. Its regular
levels are 3-1 to 3-3; its boss level is 3-4
([all-zones-mode.md](./all-zones-mode.md)). Previous: [zone
two](./zone-two.md); next: [zone four](./zone-four.md).

* **Traps:** pistons (`Piston`), teleport traps (`TeleportTrap`) and
  confusion traps (`ConfusionTrap`).
* **Enemies:** fire and ice elementals (`Fireelemental`, `Iceelemental`),
  yetis (`Yeti`), hellhounds (`Hellhound`), cauldrons (`Cauldron`,
  `Cauldron2`), slimes (`Slime4`, `Slime5`), beetles (`Beetle`, `Beetle2`),
  skeleton knights (`Skeletonknight`, `Skeletonknight2`, `Skeletonknight3`),
  goblins (`Goblin`, `Goblin2`), shove monsters (`Shovemonster`,
  `Shovemonster2`) and ghasts (`Ghast`).
