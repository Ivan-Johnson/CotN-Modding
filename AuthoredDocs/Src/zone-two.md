# Zone two

For players. The terrain, traps and enemies on zone two's regular levels in an
all-zones run that [zone one](./zone-one.md) does not cover. Its regular
levels are 2-1 to 2-3; its boss level is 2-4
([all-zones-mode.md](./all-zones-mode.md)). Next: [zone
three](./zone-three.md).

* **Terrain:** grass floor (`Grass1`, `Grass2`, `Grass3`) on every level,
  and tar (`Tar`).
* **Traps:** omni bounce traps (`BounceTrapOmni`).
* **Enemies:** mushrooms (`Mushroom`, `Mushroom2`, `MushroomExploding`,
  `MushroomLight`), fake walls (`Fakewall`), clones (`Clone`), moles
  (`Mole`), tar monsters (`Tarmonster`), armadillos (`Armadillo`,
  `Armadillo2`), armored skeletons (`Armoredskeleton`, `Armoredskeleton2`,
  `Armoredskeleton3`), skeleton mages (`Skeletonmage`, `Skeletonmage2`,
  `Skeletonmage3`), golems (`Golem`, `Golem2`) and wights (`Wight`).
