# 'Mods/Gary the Cowardly Sheep/GLOSSARY.md'

**Gary the Cowardly Sheep**:
A selectable playable character, identical to Mary except that her sheep is a coward. Mary herself is unchanged.
_Avoid_: Gary mod, Mary variant

**Gary**:
The sheep that accompanies Gary the Cowardly Sheep, in place of Mary's sheep.
_Avoid_: Lamb, pet, familiar

**Cower**:
A state Gary enters while any hostile enemy is within two tiles by taxicab distance. It does not exist in the vanilla game.
_Avoid_: Scared, afraid, frightened

**Fright zone**:
The 2x3 region of tiles centered directly opposite Gary's facing direction. It is highlighted red while Gary cowers.
_Avoid_: Behind zone, danger zone

**Death by fright**:
Gary's instant death when any entity, including Mary, occupies the Fright zone while he cowers.
_Avoid_: Scare death
